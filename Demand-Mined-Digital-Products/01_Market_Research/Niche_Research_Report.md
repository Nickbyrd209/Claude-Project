# Market Intelligence Report: 15 Digital Product Niches

**Agent:** Market Intelligence (Head of Market Research)
**Research date:** 2026-11-08
**Method:** Public web search of marketplace listing pages (Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad), vendor and competitor pages, government sources (IRS, USDA FNS, ACF, SSA) and industry reports. Nothing was scraped, no logins were used, and no access restrictions were bypassed. Each search returned summaries of public pages. I did not open every listing one by one.

## Read this first: what counts as evidence here

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Official or primary source, or a figure the platform itself publishes | IRS mileage rate; Etsy shop-page "sales" counter |
| **PROXY** | Suggests demand but is not proof of sales | Number of competing listings, review counts, "in N carts", a book on its 9th edition |
| **ESTIMATE** | Third-party tools or our own arithmetic | Sales estimates from third-party Etsy tracking sites |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | Our belief, still untested | "Providers will pay $29 for X" |

**Evidence gaps that apply to every niche:**
1. I found **no verified sales figures for individual listings**. Etsy shows sales only per shop, and I found shop-level counts only for general budget shops.
2. **Reddit threads did not come up in search.** Searches aimed at subreddits returned blogs and vendor pages instead. Customer-complaint evidence is therefore weaker than it should be, and the validation plan has to make up for it with interviews.
3. **Google Trends was not reachable** from this environment, so there is no seasonality data from it. Seasonality notes below come from the calendar of tax and compliance deadlines.
4. **The market is saturating fast (PROXY, important).** Many listings found have Etsy IDs in the 45xx–46xx million range, which means they were listed in 2026. Sellers mass-produce near-identical "Ledger | Schedule C Tax Spreadsheet" products across micro-niches: notary, landlord, child care. Reporting also describes AI-generated products flooding Etsy, and Etsy banned some AI-generated pattern and instruction listings in Oct 2026 ([Marketplace, 2026-08-14](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2026/08/14/the-ai-slopification-of-etsy); [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/etsy-bans-ai-generated-recipes-patterns/)). **Implication:** a generic tracker will not stand out. A product only wins with domain-specific calculations that a generic ledger can't do, plus demonstrations a real person would trust.

## Niche-by-niche findings

### 1. Family child care (home daycare) tax & records
- **Customer problem:** Home daycare providers can deduct a share of their home costs using a *Time-Space Percentage*. They can also use IRS standard meal rates instead of food receipts, log business miles, and they have to give each family a year-end payment statement for the parents' child care credit. Getting these calculations right is complicated and worth real money.
- **Existing solutions:** Tom Copeland's *Family Child Care Record-Keeping Guide*, a printed book now in its 9th edition, $19.95–$21.95 ([Redleaf Press](https://www.redleafpress.org/Family-Child-Care-Record-Keeping-Guide-9th-Edition-P1133.aspx); [Copeland blog](https://www.tomcopelandblog.com/blog/family-child-care-record-keeping-guide-8th-edition)). KidKare software, which its own blog says costs about $99/yr ([KidKare](https://www.tomcopelandblog.com/blog/kidkare-your-online-record-keeping-solution)). Brightwheel's paid platform. A few Etsy spreadsheets, such as a "Child Care Expense Ledger | Schedule C" ([Etsy 4562446603](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4562446603)) and a home-daycare tracker with a time-space calculator ([Etsy 4560660858](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4560660858/)).
- **Evidence of paying demand:** PROXY, moderate. A book that has gone through 9 editions plus a paid software category show people pay for help with this. Etsy listings exist but are few, and I could not see their review counts.
- **Market size:** VERIFIED. Child Care Aware counted 98,807 home-based programs in 39 reporting states in 2024. ACF counted about 121k licensed family child care programs in 2017, and numbers have been falling over the long term ([ACF](https://acf.gov/occ/news/decreasing-number-family-child-care-providers-united-states); [EdSurge 2025](https://www.edsurge.com/news/2025-06-02-report-for-first-time-in-years-home-based-child-care-programs-are-on-the-rise)). This is a focused, reachable market, but a small one.
- **Prices:** $20–22 for the book, about $99/yr for software, Etsy spreadsheet prices not confirmed.
- **Competition:** Low to moderate for spreadsheets. Moderate for software.
- **Complaints and gaps:** Vendor content says many providers miscalculate the time-space percentage and leave out prep and cleanup hours. That claim comes from KidKare and Block Advisors, both of which have an interest in saying it ([KidKare](https://www.kidkare.com/how-to-calculate-your-time-space-percentage/); [Block Advisors](https://www.blockadvisors.com/resource-center/manage-your-business/in-home-daycare-tax-deductions)). Brightwheel's marketing names manual year-end family statements and rebuilding reports at tax time as pain points ([Brightwheel](https://mybrightwheel.com/in-home-child-care/tracking-reports-manually-instead-of-in-an-all-in-one-system/)). Generic Schedule C ledgers can't do the time-space math, the meal allowance, or the per-family statements.
- **Seasonality:** Records are kept all year. Demand peaks from December to April (year-end statements and filing).
- **How buyers find products:** Facebook provider groups, state child care networks, CACFP sponsors, Pinterest, Etsy search.
- **Could an original product compete?** Yes. A spreadsheet that does these specific calculations is clearly different from generic ledgers and costs less than the software subscriptions.

### 2. Freelancer / 1099 quarterly tax set-aside tracker
- **Problem:** First-year freelancers get hit with surprise tax bills and underpayment penalties. One forum thread reports a $2,800 penalty ([terms.law forum](https://terms.law/forum/thread/estimated-tax-penalties-first-year.html); [Reddit mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rg5zbr/snapshots/2026-02-27T19%3A30%3A13.117829Z)).
- **Solutions:** Dozens of Etsy trackers priced $2–$35 ([e.g. Etsy 4514356720](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4514356720)), many free calculators ([Keeper](https://keepertax.com/self-employment-tax-rate-calculator), Bonsai, Everlance), and apps.
- **Demand:** VERIFIED market size of 74.9M U.S. independent workers by MBO's broad definition ([selfemployed.com on MBO 2026](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/mbo-partners-state-of-independence-2026/)). Evidence that people pay is weak: the visible listings were mostly "Be the first to leave a review".
- **Competition:** Very high, and the free tools are good.
- **Gap:** Weak. The differentiators left are safe-harbor logic and annualized-income timing, which are hard to explain in a short video.
- **Seasonality:** Quarterly deadlines (Apr/Jun/Sep/Jan).
- **Verdict:** Large market, saturated, crowded out by free tools.

### 3. Landlord rental property / Schedule E tracker
- **Solutions:** Many Etsy listings priced $7.80–$13 with Schedule E line mapping ([Etsy 4548146247](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4548146247); [4591436993](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4591436993)). Free software: Stessa and Baselane both have free tiers ([Baselane vs Stessa](https://www.baselane.com/resources/baselane-vs-stessa); [BiggerPockets](https://www.biggerpockets.com/posts/7275286)).
- **Demand:** PROXY, many sellers. Free software caps what people will pay.
- **Gap:** Small. "Spreadsheet vs. free app" is a hard comparison to win.

### 4. Cleaning business pricing / quoting & job-profit calculator
- **Problem:** New cleaning businesses underprice because they leave out drive time, supplies, overhead and their own time ([industry guide](https://jjandradellc.pages.dev/blog/how-to-price-cleaning-services); [Jobber community](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/cleaning-and-property-maintenance/how-to-price-deep-cleaning-services-without-undervaluing-your-work/10686)).
- **Solutions:** Etsy bundle at $13.94 ([Etsy 4565659268](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4565659268)), Payhip quote calculator at $9 ([Payhip](https://payhip.com/b/1X9uq)), free calculators from software vendors (ResponsiBid, ConvertCalculator).
- **Demand:** PROXY. Several sellers. "Cleaning TikTok" is a large organic channel, but that is my observation and has not been measured.
- **Gap:** Moderate. A quote that adds a margin check ("this quote pays you $X/hour after costs") is easy to show in a demo.

### 5. Mobile notary / loan signing agent fee & mileage tracker
- **Solutions:** A $19 7-sheet tracker, a $49 Notion system, and Schedule C ledgers ([Etsy 4601073172](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4601073172); [4601046015](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4601046015)), plus apps (NotaryGadget, NotaryKit).
- **Demand:** PROXY. Listing prices up to $49 suggest people will pay for specialized tools. There are many 2026 listings.
- **Gap:** Low to moderate. Apps already cover the core job.

### 6. PTA / PTO treasurer kit
- **Solutions:** Etsy listings at $4.99–$10, including an HTML dashboard ([Etsy 4586556141](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4586556141)). Free official templates from PTA.org, state PTAs and FutureFund ([PTA.org](https://www.pta.org/local-leader-kit/finance/templates-tools-resources); [VAPTA](https://vapta.org/?p=1368)).
- **Demand:** PROXY, low prices. Free official tools compete directly.
- **Gap:** Volunteers worry about being liable (see [PTO Today "scary stories"](https://www.ptotoday.com/pto-today-articles/scary-stories-every-parent-group-treasurer-knows-and-how-to-avoid-them)). There may be an opening for a "handover-proof" audit kit.
- **Seasonality:** Strong. Treasurers change over in Aug–Sep.

### 7. Small nonprofit grant & donor tracker
- **Solutions:** Etsy listings at $2.99–$21.36 ([Etsy 4512291854](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4512291854)). Strong free alternatives from Neon One, Keela, Zeffy, Jotform, Baserow and Grist ([Neon One](https://neonone.com/resources/guide/donor-database-template/); [Keela](https://www.keela.co/free-excel-donation-spreadsheet-template)).
- **Gap:** Small. CRM vendors give away good templates to win customers.

### 8. Personal budget spreadsheets (general)
- **Demand:** **VERIFIED (shop level)**. Etsy shop pages show 93,181 sales for Haye Creatives and 177,462 for PrioriDigitalStudio, with more than 10k reviews each ([Haye](https://www.etsy.com/shop/hayeameri); [Priori](https://etsy.com/shop/PrioriDigitalStudio); [TheWeeklyCrew](https://www.etsy.com/ca/shop/TheWeeklyCrew)). This is the strongest proof of paying demand found for any niche. Shop totals cover all their products, not one listing.
- **Competition:** Extreme. Established shops with tens of thousands of reviews dominate search results.
- **Gap:** Very small. Price competition is exactly what the brief tells us to avoid.

### 9. Wedding budget planner
- **Solutions:** Many listings at $1.41–$38.85, mostly $3–15. One Shop.app listing has 100+ reviews ([Shop.app](https://shop.app/products/6708079722569/wedding-planning-spreadsheet-excel-template)).
- **Gap:** Small. Buyers usually purchase once, so there is no repeat business.

### 10. Job application tracker
- **Solutions:** Listings at $2–$11 with few reviews, plus free Notion templates ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/templates/job-tracker-wimpel)).
- **Gap:** Very small. Free alternatives work well enough.

### 11. Short-term rental (Airbnb) host profit tracker
- **Solutions:** Etsy listings at $3.49–$22.96 ([Etsy 4415865110](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4415865110)), Someka, financialaha, and full software (Guesty and others).
- **Gap:** Moderate. Hosts care about KPIs like occupancy, ADR and RevPAR, but many trackers already include them.

### 12. Handyman / contractor estimating & job costing
- **Solutions:** A $25.21 handyman price book with 118 jobs, a $36.33 GC bid workbook, and a $109 bundle of 17 templates ([Etsy 4582251962](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4582251962)). Free templates from Housecall Pro, Shiftflow and Invoicer.
- **Demand:** PROXY. Higher prices than other niches suggest buyers value these tools more.
- **Gap:** Moderate. Building a credible price book requires trade knowledge we don't have.

### 13. Family caregiver binder (aging parents)
- **Solutions:** Many printable and fillable PDFs at $1–$15, including dementia-specific binders ([Etsy 4582000090](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4582000090); [4509768587](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4509768587)).
- **Problem severity:** High, because the stakes are emotional and medical. Prices are low and the listings show "Be the first to leave a review".
- **Gap:** Moderate, mostly on organization and on sharing between siblings. Medical content needs careful limits on what we claim.

### 14. Debt payoff (snowball/avalanche) planner
- **Demand:** ESTIMATE. A third-party tracker estimates one shop's calculator at 7,105 sales and 988 reviews at about $5 ([findniche](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/debt-snowball-avalanche-calculator-sprea-1332624229)). This is not Etsy's own data.
- **Gap:** Small. The market is saturated with low prices, and free calculators exist (e.g. Undebt.it).

### 15. Gig / delivery driver earnings, mileage & tax tracker
- **Solutions:** Etsy listings at $1–$27, a $49 Payhip/Gumroad tracker, a free Shoeboxed sheet, and the Gridwise app ([Gridwise](https://gridwise.io/features/earnings); [Shoeboxed](https://register.shoeboxed.com/blog/uber-driver-expenses-spreadsheet)).
- **Gap:** Low to moderate. Mileage apps track automatically, and a spreadsheet can't.

*Also noted but not ranked: Etsy-seller pricing and profit trackers. These compete with Craftybase software and many listings ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/etsy-accounting-software)).*
