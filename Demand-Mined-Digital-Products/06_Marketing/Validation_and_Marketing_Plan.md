## Timing advantage
Today is Nov 8. Providers prepare year-end family statements and gather records in **December and January**, and file from February to April. A 3–4 week validation sprint finishing by early December lands right at the start of peak need.

## Part A: Validation plan (Phase 3)

| # | What we test | Method | Cost | Pass threshold | Kill / pivot threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Do providers recognize the problem? | 8–10 twenty-minute interviews (script below), recruited with admin permission from Facebook provider groups, or through a local child care resource & referral agency or CACFP sponsor | $50 in gift cards | ≥6 of 10 say they find time-space %, meal method, or parent statements confusing or time-consuming | ≤3 of 10 |
| V2 | Do current solutions leave a gap? | Same interviews: "What do you use today? What's annoying about it?" | — | ≥5 are not on paid software and not satisfied with what they use now | Most are happy KidKare/Brightwheel users |
| V3 | Does our product close the gap? | Screen-share the prototype, or send the file to 5 interviewees and have them enter last week's numbers | — | ≥4 of 5 finish setup and a week's entry in under 20 minutes without help | ≤2 of 5 |
| V4 | Is the price reasonable? | Ask the 4 Van Westendorp price questions, then make a **real offer** | — | Median "getting expensive" ≥ $29 | Median "too expensive" < $19 |
| V5 | Real purchase intent | Payhip early-access page at $19 (honest founding price). Announced through approved posts and the free calculator email list | $0 | ≥10 paid orders **or** ≥3% conversion on ≥300 qualified visitors within 21 days | <3 orders from ≥300 qualified visitors |
| V6 | Could CAC work? | Organic only at first. Optional paid test (≤$34) only if V5 is inconclusive | $0–34 | Organic sales occur. If paid: CAC < $15 | Paid CAC > $30 |

We are not counting survey "would you buy?" answers as validation. Only V5 (real money) can move the status to **GO**.

### Interview script (20 min)
1. Tell me about your program: how many children, what hours, how long you've been running it.
2. Walk me through what you did for taxes last year. Who prepared them? What did you bring them?
3. How did you work out the share of home costs you could deduct? *(Listen for mentions of time-space.)*
4. Food: did you use receipts or the standard meal rates? How did you decide?
5. How do you give families their year-end payment info? How long does it take?
6. What do you use to keep records today? What do you like or dislike about it? What does it cost?
7. *(Show prototype)* What's confusing here? What's missing?
8. Price questions: at what price is this so cheap you'd doubt it? A bargain? Getting expensive? Too expensive?
9. Can I send you a link when it's ready? *(Their consent is needed before storing their contact details.)*

## Part B: Marketing plan

### Ideal customer
A home child care provider (U.S.), often running the business alone, who is good with kids and dreads paperwork. They're active in Facebook provider groups. Their most frustrating problem: *"I know I'm supposed to track hours and meals for taxes, but I never know if I'm doing it right, and January is a nightmare."* (Hypothesis, to be confirmed in V1.)

### Value proposition
**"Track 15 minutes a week. In January, hand your preparer one page and print every family's statement in minutes."**

### Five organic marketing concepts (demonstrations, not hype)
| # | Concept | What the viewer sees | Hook (on-screen text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 30-second Time-Space %** | Screen recording: type 2,000 sq ft and 1,500 sq ft, enter weekly hours, and 28.7% appears | "Your Time-Space % in 30 seconds (no math)" |
| 2 | **Prep time counts** | Toggle prep/cleanup hours from 0 to 12 per week and watch the % rise | "Do you count your Sunday meal prep? You may be allowed to." |
| 3 | **Meal allowance vs. receipts** | Flip the Food method dropdown and show both totals side by side | "Receipts or standard meal rates? See both." |
| 4 | **January statements in minutes** | Pick a family, print the statement, repeat | "Parent tax statements: pick a name and print" |
| 5 | **The mid-year mileage change** | Two trips, one in June and one in July, at different rates, filled in automatically | "The IRS changed the mileage rate mid-2026. Did you notice?" |

All examples use fictional data and are labeled "example". No invented outcomes, no "save $X,000" claims, and every video ends with "Not tax advice; check with your preparer".

### Short-form video scripts (≈30–45 s each)
**Script 1: "Time-Space % in 30 seconds"**
- 0–3 s: Hook text over a spreadsheet: "Home daycare providers: your Time-Space % in 30 seconds."
- 3–15 s: VO: "It's the share of your rent or mortgage interest, utilities and insurance you may be able to deduct. You need two things: square footage and hours." Type the numbers.
- 15–25 s: VO: "Most people forget prep and cleanup time. Add it here." Number updates.
- 25–35 s: VO: "That's your percentage, ready for your preparer. Template link in bio. Not tax advice."

**Script 2: "Do you count prep time?"**
- Hook: "If you meal-prep on Sundays for your daycare kids, watch this."
- Body: Show the Time Log with prep hours at 0 and then at 12 per week, and the % changing. VO cites "IRS Publication 587 lets you count time spent in the home on business, like cleaning and prep". *(Exact wording to be confirmed by the CPA/EA review.)*
- CTA: "Free Time-Space calculator, link in bio."

**Script 3: "Receipts or meal rates?"**
- Hook: "Two ways to deduct daycare food. Here's both side by side."
- Body: Meals tab counts, then the Tax Summary comparison, then flip the dropdown.
- CTA: "Ask your preparer which fits you. Calculator in bio."

**Script 4: "Parent statements"**
- Hook: "January = every parent asks for their child care statement."
- Body: Choose a family, the statement fills in, print. Repeat for 3 families in about 15 seconds.
- CTA: "Workbook link in bio."

**Script 5: "Mileage rate changed mid-year"**
- Hook: "The 2026 IRS mileage rate changed on July 1."
- Body: Show the trip rows switching from 72.5¢ to 76¢. *(Only after confirming 76¢ on irs.gov.)*
- CTA: "Track trips for daycare groceries and supplies. Link in bio."

### Customer education strategy
A free **Time-Space % Calculator** (one tab, email opt-in through Payhip), followed by a 4-email series: (1) how time-space works, (2) what counts as business hours, (3) meal methods, (4) January checklist plus the workbook offer. The emails educate first; the offer comes in email 4 only. Requires owner approval and an email provider (Payhip includes basic email).
