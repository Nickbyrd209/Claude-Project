## Quality gates (what has to be true before a product moves on)
1. **To Prototype:** Score ≥65 and a gap that can be stated in one sentence and demonstrated.
2. **To Customer Testing:** 0 formula errors, independent-model tests passing, opens correctly in Excel and Google Sheets.
3. **To Ready for Approval:** V1–V3 pass, professional review done (for tax/legal-adjacent products), and **real money from V5**.
4. **To Active:** Owner launch approval, product page reviewed, refund and support workflow ready.
