## 5. Support, refund & feedback workflow
- **Refunds:** 14-day "if it doesn't work for your situation" refund on request, no questions asked. Many template sellers refuse refunds on digital files. Offering one lowers risk for a cautious buyer and builds trust. Assumed refund rate: 3–5%.
- **Support:** One email inbox. Saved replies for the 5 most likely questions (Google Sheets import, Alaska/Hawaii rates, deleting example rows, where to find depreciation, "is this tax advice?"). Target response time: under 2 business days. The owner handles support, since I can't send email.
- **Feedback:** Automated Payhip follow-up email at day 7 with 3 questions: "What was confusing?", "What's missing?", "Would you buy the 2027 edition?". Results go into the `10_Weekly_Reports` log. No real customer data is processed without approval.

## 6. Performance reporting
Weekly: visitors, sales, conversion %, refunds, support tickets, and top questions, entered on the `Financial Performance` tab of the dashboard. Monthly: contribution margin, CAC (if any paid spend), and the 3 most common pieces of feedback.

## 7. Sample product page (draft — not published)

**Title:** Home Daycare Tax Records Workbook 2026 — Automatic Time-Space %, Meal Allowance & Parent Statements (Excel / Google Sheets)

**Subtitle:** Built for family child care providers. Log your hours, meals, receipts and miles each week. In January, hand your preparer one clear summary and print every family's statement in minutes.

**What it does for you**
- **Calculates your Time-Space Percentage for you.** Enter your square footage and weekly hours, including prep and cleanup time, and the percentage updates itself.
- **Compares meal methods.** See the IRS standard meal allowance next to your actual food receipts.
- **Sorts expenses correctly.** Pick "business only" or "shared", and shared costs are multiplied by your Time-Space % automatically.
- **Tracks mileage at the 2026 IRS rates,** including the mid-year rate change.
- **Prints year-end statements for parents.** Pick a family and print.
- **Shows a rough quarterly set-aside,** so tax time isn't a shock.

**What's inside:** 10 tabs: Start Here, Setup, Families, Time Log, Income, Expenses, Meals, Mileage, Tax Summary, Parent Statements. Example rows and color-coded "type here" cells are included.

**Who it's for:** U.S. home-based (family) child care providers who file Schedule C. Not designed for child care centers.

**Honest limits:** This is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice, and it doesn't file anything. Depreciation and the Form 8829 limit are flagged for your preparer, not calculated. Rates are 2026 rates; edit them on the Setup tab if you're in Alaska or Hawaii.

**Format:** One .xlsx file. Works in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets (File → Import). *[Google Sheets compatibility to be confirmed before publishing.]*

**Refunds:** If it doesn't fit your situation, email within 14 days for a full refund.
